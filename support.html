Do I need to save a game?

Game changes are saved on this iPhone as you make them. On each signing screen, tap “Save signature” to commit that coach’s name and drawing. An unfinished drawing is not a saved signature. Return to the game list to reopen a draft or signed game.

How do I create the signed report?

Finish the game, review both teams, and have both head coaches save their signatures. Tap “Create signed PDF & Share” and choose how to save or send the PDF. Sharing may require an internet connection depending on your chosen app.

Why did signatures disappear after editing?

Unlocking a signed game clears both signatures. The revised counts need both coaches’ signoff again.

Where are my records?

Game files and generated PDFs are stored locally in the app. There is no app-managed cloud account or synchronization. Keep exported copies of reports you need. Deleting a game does not delete previously generated or shared PDFs. Deleting the app can remove its local records; offloading is different and preserves documents. Device backups may contain copies depending on your settings.

Does this calculate rest or certify eligibility?

No. The report is unofficial. Check your league’s pitching rules, official record requirements and eligibility procedures separately.
