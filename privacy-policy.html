Pitch Counter: Signed Game Log

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Pitch Counter: Signed Game Log is provided by Kyle Jones. This policy describes version 1.2’s game-recording features and the optional Pitch Counter Pro purchase.

Information you enter

You can enter game dates and levels, team names, pitcher names, jersey numbers and pitch totals. Head coaches can enter their names and draw signatures. These details and the report’s signing times and check code are used to create, save, review and export your game record. Signature drawings are report content; the app does not use them for biometric identification.

Storage and developer access

The app stores game records and generated PDF reports in its local document storage on your iPhone. It does not require an account and does not send those records to a developer-operated server. The app has no developer-operated advertising or analytics service, and the developer does not sell game data or use it for cross-app tracking.

In-app purchase

Pitch Counter Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive your payment details. The app keeps a record on your iPhone that Pro is unlocked and how many of the free signed reports have been used, and asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns Pro so that Restore Purchases works. This information is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Sharing you choose

A PDF can contain player and team information, coach names, handwritten signatures and signing times. When you use the share sheet, the destination app, service and recipients you choose receive that PDF under their own privacy practices. Review the contents and obtain appropriate permission before sharing someone else’s information. The app does not automatically send reports to the developer, a league or another coach.

Device services and backups

Apple’s operating system, device backup settings and distribution services may process information under Apple’s policies. Local app documents may be included in device backups according to your settings. The app does not provide its own cloud backup or cross-device synchronization. TestFlight testing may involve Apple’s beta feedback and diagnostic services.

Retention and deletion

Game records remain locally until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Deleting a game from the game list removes that saved game record, but does not remove previously generated PDF files from the app’s PDF storage or any copies already saved or shared elsewhere. Deleting the app through iPhone Settings removes its local app container; offloading the app preserves documents and data. Device backups and externally shared copies may remain. Manage those copies and backups through the relevant Apple settings, storage services and recipients. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete local game records.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and anything you attach, and uses them to answer your request. Please use fictional player details in examples and do not send children’s personal information or signatures unless necessary and permitted. Correspondence is handled through the email provider. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence; copies may remain in provider backups or where retention is required.

Children and team information

This recordkeeping app is intended for adult coaches and scorekeepers. It does not create child accounts or provide public profiles, messaging or a social feed. Adults entering or sharing player information should follow their organization’s requirements and obtain any necessary permission.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. For support or privacy questions, email kajonesco@gmail.com.
